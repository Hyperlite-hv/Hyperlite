Alert rules: administrators write rules such as "the processor of a VM above 85 % for 10 minutes", scoped to one node, one VM, every node or every VM, on the processor, memory, disk or network. The cluster's leader evaluates them after each sample on the average over the window, notifies once when a rule fires for a resource and again when it resolves (to the channels the rule names, or to every channel taking the new "Alert rule" event), and writes both to the journal. The firing state is replicated, so every node shows it. A new Alert rules service lists the rules with their state.
