A weekly report sums up the last seven days: backups succeeded and failed, each node's processor and memory load, the busiest VMs, the fullest storage pools, the alerts and the failed operations. It goes to the notification channels that take the new "Weekly report" event, on the weekday and hour an administrator chooses. The cluster's leader sends it, once a week. The Notifications page sets it up, previews it and can send it at once.
