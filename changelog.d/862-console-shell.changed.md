- The interface is rebuilt as a console (#862): a header band with the search, the notifications and the account, a menu of services on the left with the ones you pin, and a Home page with the services, the resources opened recently, the state of the cluster and the running operations. Every service page opens with a breadcrumb, its title and a command bar; a machine, a container or a node opens on its own page, with its pages in a menu on its left. "All services" lists every service by family, "All resources" every machine, container and node in one list. The colours, the typeface (Inter) and the mark are new, and the theme follows the system setting unless you choose light or dark. Every page address and every `?tab=` link keeps working.
